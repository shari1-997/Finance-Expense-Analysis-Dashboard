# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Provide useful, specific feedback.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Respect different perspectives.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, or intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting

For concerns, contact the repository maintainer through the contact method provided on the GitHub profile.
